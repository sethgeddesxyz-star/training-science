# Bodyweight Exercise Ladders

Eight progression ladders for bodyweight exercises. Each ladder is an ordered sequence of steps from easiest to hardest. These are used when an exercise is in **ladder mode** (no external load, progression through difficulty).

---

## General Rules

- **Advance threshold**: 3 × 8 clean reps (or 3 × 30s for holds) → move to next step at 3 × 5
- **Entry gate**: If you can't hit 3 × 5 at the new step within 1-2 weeks, regress to the previous step and consolidate at the advance standard
- **Stall limit**: 3 sessions with no progress → apply a gap bridge method
- **Layoff rule**: > 14 days without training → regress one step, restart at the start target
- **Pain rule**: If an exercise causes pain, immediately regress one step. If pain repeats, persistent regression.
- **Deload**: 65% volume (e.g., 2 × 5 instead of 3 × 8)

## Ladder 1: Push-Up (Horizontal Push)

| Step | Exercise | Advance At | Start At |
|------|----------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | Wall push-up | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 2 | Incline push-up (hip height) | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 3 | Incline push-up (knee height) | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 4 | Knee push-up (optional) | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 5 | Standard push-up | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 6 | Diamond push-up | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 7 | Decline push-up (feet elevated 12-24") | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 8 | Pseudo-planche push-up (hands at waist, forward lean) | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 9 | Archer push-up | 3 × 6/side | 3 × 3/side |
| 10 | Incline one-arm push-up (hand on box, lower over time) | 3 × 5/side | 3 × 3/side |
| 11 | One-arm push-up (terminal) | Build reps | 3 × 3/side |

**Gap bridges**: Negatives (slow lower from top), partial ROM, band-assisted one-arm

## Ladder 2: Pull-Up (Vertical Pull)

| Step | Exercise | Advance At | Start At |
|------|----------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | Dead hang | 3 × 30s | 3 × 10s |
| 2 | Scapular pulls (shoulder-blade-only retraction) | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 3 | Arch hang / active hang | 3 × 30s | 3 × 15s |
| 4 | Negative pull-ups (jump up, 3-5s controlled lower) | 3 × 6-8 (5s+ eccentric) | 3 × 3 |
| 5 | Band-assisted pull-ups | 3 × 8 (light band) | 3 × 5 |
| 6 | Full pull-up | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 7 | Weighted pull-up (+2.5-5 lb increments) | 3 × 5 @ +20-30% BW | — |
| 8 | Archer pull-up | 3 × 5/side | 3 × 3/side |
| 9 | One-arm pull-up progressions (towel/finger assist) | Terminal | — |

**Gap bridges**: Grease the groove (frequent submaximal sets throughout the day), negative-only sets, band-assisted at harder step

## Ladder 3: Inverted Row (Horizontal Pull)

| Step | Exercise | Advance At | Start At |
|------|----------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | Vertical row (standing, slight lean back) | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 2 | Incline row (~45° angle) | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 3 | Horizontal row (body straight, heels on floor) | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 4 | Wide-grip or tuck-feet row | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 5 | Feet-elevated row (fully horizontal) | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 6 | Weighted or archer row (terminal) | Build reps | 3 × 5 |

**Key**: Lower bar/rings = harder. Feet elevated = hardest.

## Ladder 5: Squat (Knee-Dominant)

| Step | Exercise | Advance At | Start At |
|------|----------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | Assisted squat (holding pole/doorframe) | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 2 | Air squat (full ROM) | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 3 | Split squat | 3 × 8/side | 3 × 5/side |
| 4 | Bulgarian split squat (rear foot elevated) | 3 × 8/side | 3 × 5/side |
| 5 | Beginner shrimp squat (rear foot touching down) | 3 × 8/side | 3 × 5/side |
| 6 | Intermediate shrimp squat (rear foot held at glute) | 3 × 8/side | 3 × 5/side |
| 7 | Advanced shrimp squat / box pistol | 3 × 5-8/side | 3 × 3/side |
| 8 | Assisted pistol squat (TRX/pole) | 3 × 5/side | 3 × 3/side |
| 9 | Full pistol squat (terminal) | Build reps | 3 × 3/side |

**Prerequisite for pistol**: ~20 clean air squats + adequate ankle mobility.
**⚠️ Knee flag**: Use controlled tempos at every step. If pain, hold current step or regress.

## Ladder 6: Hinge (Hip-Dominant / Hamstrings)

| Step | Exercise | Advance At | Start At |
|------|----------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | Glute bridge (two legs) | 3 × 10-12 | 3 × 8 |
| 2 | Single-leg glute bridge | 3 × 8/side | 3 × 5/side |
| 3 | Hip thrust (shoulders on bench) / SL hip thrust | 3 × 8/side | 3 × 5/side |
| 4 | DB Romanian deadlift (hybrid — uses load) | Transition to external load progression | — |
| 5 | Assisted Nordic curl (band or hand pushoff) | 3 × 5 (decreasing assist) | 3 × 3 |
| 6 | Nordic curl negatives (slow lower, push back up) | 3 × 5 (slow eccentrics) | 3 × 3 |
| 7 | Full Nordic hamstring curl (terminal) | Build reps | 3 × 3 |

**Key**: Pair knee-flexion work (Nordics) with hip-extension work (bridges/RDL) — both hamstring functions.

## Ladder 7: Handstand / Overhead Press (Vertical Push)

| Step | Exercise | Advance At | Start At |
|------|----------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | Pike push-up (floor) | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 2 | Elevated pike push-up (feet on box) | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 3 | Wall handstand hold (chest to wall) | 60s hold | 3 × 15s |
| 4 | Wall HSPU, partial ROM (half depth) | 3 × 5 | 3 × 2 |
| 5 | Wall HSPU, full ROM (head to floor/books) | 3 × 5 | 3 × 2 |
| 6 | Freestanding handstand hold (parallel skill) | 30s+ hold | — |
| 7 | Freestanding HSPU / deficit wall HSPU (terminal) | Build reps | 3 × 1 |

**Gate**: Step 3 (wall hold, 60s chest-to-wall) is REQUIRED before attempting pressing steps.
**ROM trick**: Stack books/plates under head to gradually increase depth.
**⚠️ Shoulder flag**: Progress cautiously. Ensure pain-free wall holds before any pressing.

| Step | Exercise | Advance At | Start At |
|------|----------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | Plank | 3 × 60s (then add difficulty, not time) | 3 × 20s |
| 2 | Hollow body hold (tucked → straddle → full) | 3 × 30-60s | 3 × 15s |
| 3 | Reverse crunch | 3 × 10-12 | 3 × 6 |
| 4 | Dead bug / hardstyle plank | 3 × 30s | 3 × 15s |
| 5 | Tuck dragon flag (knees bent) | 3 × 5-8 | 3 × 3 |
| 6 | Single-leg / straddle dragon flag | 3 × 5 | 3 × 3 |
| 7 | Dragon flag negatives (slow lower only) | 3 × 5 (5s eccentric) | 3 × 2 |
| 8 | Full dragon flag (terminal) | Build reps | 3 × 2 |

## Gap Bridge Methods

When stalled for 3+ sessions at any step, apply ONE of these methods (in preference order, chosen based on the specific step):

1. **Grease the Groove (GtG)**: Multiple submaximal sets spread throughout the day (e.g., 50% of max every 1-2 hours)
2. **Partial ROM**: Reduced range-of-motion reps at the harder step
3. **Hybrid sets**: Final set at harder variation + remaining reps at easier variation
4. **Eccentric-only (negatives)**: Slow controlled lowering at the harder step
5. **Micro-assistance**: Bands, one-finger spot, elevation changes to reduce difficulty slightly

After 1-2 weeks of bridge work, re-test the full step.
