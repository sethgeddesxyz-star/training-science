# Kettlebell Repertoire (added 2026-10-18)

Kettlebells serve the SBS program as (a) home/anywhere load for the hinge, squat, press, pull
and carry patterns; (b) the `KB` modality family for the Accessory Rotation
(`sbs-progression-rules.md` §6, `modality-rotation.md`); (c) the engine of several quick
sessions (`quick-sessions.md`); (d) the home variant of HIIT Format B (`conditioning-hiit.md`
§3). A kettlebell lift **never replaces a main-lift tier** except as the home substitute in §7.
Per-user state: `users/<name>/training-state.md` **## Kettlebell** (§8). The user's
`injury-protocols.md` always wins over this file.

---

## 1. The science, briefly (references in §11)

- **Swings build power; the strength and aerobic evidence is unclear.** Six weeks of swings
  raised half-squat 1RM ~10% and vertical jump ~20% (Lake & Lauder 2012a); four weeks matched
  explosive deadlifts (Maulit 2017); but against conventional weightlifting the barbell group
  gained more strength (Otto 2012). Review verdict: supported for power, unclear for strength
  and aerobic fitness (Beardsley & Contreras 2014).
- **Why the swing works: impulse.** A 32 kg swing out-produced a 60% 1RM back squat for net
  impulse, and demand rose with bell mass (Lake & Lauder 2012b). Hence §2: progress a swing by
  bell first, reps second.
- **It is a hip-extension movement.** Semitendinosus 73-115% MVC (Zebis 2013), glutes ~80% MVC
  (McGill & Marshall 2012). Cue **"float, not lift"**: hips throw, arms are ropes. A swing
  lifted with the shoulders is no longer a hinge.
- **Spine load is real and oddly shaped.** ~3,200 N L4/L5 compression at 16 kg with
  **posterior** shear, the reverse of a conventional lift; "may not suit every back" (McGill &
  Marshall 2012). Hence §3: every ballistic closes while a back flag is open.
- **The swing is the back-extensor-heaviest ballistic;** clean and snatch shift work to the
  trunk and arm (Lyons 2017).
- **Conditioning is legitimate.** Continuous 16 kg swings sit at ~65% VO2max / ~87% HRmax
  (Farrar 2010); snatch intervals raised VO2max ~6% (Falatic 2015); a kettlebell Tabata
  out-cost sprint cycling (Williams & Kraemer 2015). So swings/snatches are a HIIT Format B
  home variant, not a lifting substitute.
- **Pain and older adults.** Ballistic KB work cut neck/shoulder and low-back pain in workers
  (Jay 2011, 2013); hardstyle training in 59-79 year-olds was well tolerated and improved
  function (Meigh 2022). The field's scoping review calls the longitudinal evidence
  underpowered and low quality (Meigh 2019).
- **Press.** No EMG difference from a dumbbell press at matched load (Blazkiewicz & Hadamus
  2022): treat it as an offset-load DB press; bottoms-up is a stability test, not a builder.
- **Caveat.** Trials are 4-10 weeks, n = 8-40, novices, several from one group. The §5
  benchmarks are coaching standards, not trial outcomes. The case here is practical: one
  implement loads five patterns anywhere, and the swing is the best-evidenced home hinge.

## 2. Bells and increments

| kg | 8 | 12 | 16 | 20 | 24 | 28 | 32 | 36 | 40 | 48 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| lb (approx) | 18 | 26 | 35 | 44 | 53 | 62 | 71 | 79 | 88 | 106 |

Log the bell in the unit stamped on it. A bell step is +25-33%, far wider than the ~5% the §3
double-progression window assumes, so the window is widened:

1. **Extended window:** grinds (press, squat, row, deadlift, floor press, lunge) **8-15**;
   ballistics (swing, clean, snatch, high pull) **10-20**. Get-up and carries use their §4
   ladders, not reps.
2. **Advance** = all sets at the window top, effort not "easy", no form flag → next bell, reps
   reset to the floor.
3. The **§3 forced-increment exception applies:** the first session at the new bell below the
   floor is not a failure.
4. **No next bell:** run the §5 Ceiling Test first: (a) paired bells, (b) mixed DB + KB, (c)
   unilateral conversion (2H → 1H swing, goblet → single rack). Then the ceiling ladder (Rung
   1 widens ballistics to 15-25).
5. **Mixed pair:** double lifts only, one size apart, swap sides each set, log `2 × 16/20 kg`.
   Never for the swing.

**Starting bell** (the §2 "Unknown exercise" calibration gate; first session only, adjust in
session):

| Lift | Men | Women | In-session check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Two-hand swing | 16 kg | 12 kg | 10 reps float to chest height, no shoulder pull; "easy" and crisp = +1 bell now |
| One-hand swing, clean, high pull | 16 kg | 12 kg | shoulders square; soft forearm landing on the clean |
| Snatch | 12-16 kg | 8-12 kg | 5 clean reps/side, no forearm bang |
| Press | 12-16 kg | 8 kg | 5 strict reps/side, ribs down |
| Goblet squat | 16 kg | 12 kg | full depth, heels down, 8 reps |
| Deadlift / suitcase deadlift | 24 kg | 16 kg | neutral spine, 8 reps |
| Get-up | shoe on fist | shoe on fist | §4 Get-up ladder; first bell 8-12 kg |
| Carries | ~25% BW per hand | ~25% BW per hand | 40 m without lean or grip loss |

### Flag routing (generic; the user's `injury-protocols.md` decides specifics)

- **Shoulder:** snatch, overhead/waiter carry, windmill, bent press and bottoms-up carry wait
  for Get-up Step 4; swings stay two-hand; press runs half-kneeling until the same gate clears.
- **Knee:** racked lunges and full-depth front squats follow the `bodyweight-ladders.md` squat
  ladder gating (box depth first, controlled tempo, regress one step on any sensation). Swings
  and deadlifts are unaffected.
- **Back:** while a back Pain Response Protocol is open, **no swings, cleans, snatches or high
  pulls**; deadlift and carries only, at calibration loads, pain-free. Closing the protocol
  reopens ballistics one ladder step **below** where they were left.

## 4. Progression ladders

`bodyweight-ladders.md` general rules apply (entry gate, stall limit 3 sessions, layoff > 14
days = regress one step, pain = regress one step). "Crisp" = hip snap, bell floats, no shoulder
pull, no rounding, no grip failure; one ugly rep ends the set and the set does not count. At
the terminal step a lift leaves ladder mode for §2 double progression; the step stays in state
for regression.

### Swing ladder

| Step | Exercise | Advance At | Start At |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | KB deadlift, neutral spine, bell between the feet | 3 × 10 | 3 × 5 |
| 2 | Hike-pass and park (hike back, stand, park dead) | 3 × 5 clean hikes | 3 × 3 |
| 3 | Two-hand swing, chest height | 5 × 10 crisp | 3 × 10 |
| 4 | One-hand swing, shoulders square | 5 × 10/side crisp | 3 × 5/side |
| 5 | Hand-to-hand swing | 3 × 20 alternating | 3 × 10 |
| 6 | Heavier bell: return to Step 3 at the next bell (loop until no next bell) | Step 3 standard | 3 × 10 |
| 7 | Dead-stop swing at the heaviest two-hand bell (terminal, power) | 5 × 5 | 3 × 5 |

Gap bridges: towel swing (forces the float), band-around-the-bell swing, deadlift-to-swing
hybrid sets.

### Clean ladder

| Step | Exercise | Advance At | Start At |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One-hand swing at the clean bell (Swing Step 4) | 5 × 10/side | — |
| 2 | Dead clean from the floor, soft forearm landing | 3 × 5/side | 3 × 3/side |
| 3 | Swing clean, no forearm bang, elbow tucked | 5 × 5/side | 3 × 3/side |
| 4 | Double clean, two bells | 5 × 5 | 3 × 3 |
| 5 | Heavier bell: return to Step 3 (terminal loop) | Step 3 standard | 3 × 3/side |

### Snatch ladder

| Step | Exercise | Advance At | Start At |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One-hand swing at the snatch bell (Swing Step 4) | 5 × 10/side | — |
| 2 | High pull: elbow high and out, bell at shoulder height, arc close to the body | 5 × 5/side | 3 × 5/side |
| 3 | Swing → high pull → punch drill, third rep punched to a soft lockout | 5 × 3/side, no forearm contact | 3 × 2/side |
| 4 | Snatch, drop by hiking (never lowering through the front) | 5 × 5/side | 3 × 3/side |
| 5 | Snatch volume: 10 × 10 alternating in 10 min | all 100 | 10 × 5 |
| 6 | Heavier bell: return to Step 3 (terminal loop) | Step 3 standard | 3 × 2/side |

Shoulder flag: Step 3 onward requires Get-up Step 4. Back flag: whole ladder closed.

### Get-up ladder

| Step | Exercise | Advance At | Start At |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Naked: roll to elbow, back down | 5/side smooth | 3/side |
| 2 | Naked: to hand, to bridge (or low sweep), back down | 5/side | 3/side |
| 3 | Naked: to kneel (leg sweep, windshield wiper, half-kneel), back down | 5/side | 3/side |
| 4 | Naked: full get-up to stand and back, eyes on the fist | 5/side | 3/side |
| 5 | Shoe on fist, full get-up, shoe never falls | 5/side | 3/side |
| 6 | Light bell (8-12 kg), full get-up, 30-40 s per rep | 5/side | 2/side |
| 7 | Heavier bell: 1/side at the new bell + the rest at the old, +1/side per session | 5/side at the new bell | 1/side |

Step 4 is **the shoulder gate** used throughout §3. Pain on the way up ends that rep and
regresses the ladder one step for the session.

### Press ladder

| Step | Exercise | Advance At | Start At |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Half-kneeling press (rear knee down, bell side) | 3 × 8/side | 3 × 5/side |
| 2 | Standing one-arm press from a clean, ribs down, biceps by the ear at lockout | 3 × 8/side | 3 × 5/side |
| 3 | Double progression on Step 2 (8-15 window, §2) until no next bell | — | — |
| 4 | Push press at the next bell (dip-drive, strict lower) as the overload step | 3 × 8/side | 3 × 5/side |
| 5 | Double press, two bells, from a double clean | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| P | Parallel: bottoms-up press at 1-2 bells lighter | 3 × 5/side without tipping | 3 × 3/side |

### Front squat ladder

| Step | Exercise | Advance At | Start At |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Goblet squat, full depth, heels down, elbows inside the knees | 3 × 10 | 3 × 5 |
| 2 | Single-rack front squat, no lateral lean | 3 × 8/side | 3 × 5/side |
| 3 | Double-rack front squat | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 4 | Double progression on Step 3 (8-15 window); racked reverse lunge as the unilateral swap at the ceiling | — | — |

Knee flag: every step starts at box depth and earns full depth as the squat ladder does.

### Carry ladder

| Step | Exercise | Advance At | Start At |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Farmer carry, two bells, ~25% BW per hand | 2 × 40 m, no grip loss | 2 × 20 m |
| 2 | Suitcase carry, ~25-30% BW, hips and shoulders level | 2 × 40 m/side | 2 × 20 m/side |
| 3 | Rack carry, one bell, ribs down | 2 × 40 m/side | 2 × 20 m/side |
| 4 | Waiter (overhead) carry, elbow locked, biceps by the ear (shoulder gate) | 2 × 20 m/side | 2 × 10 m/side |
| 5 | Bottoms-up carry (shoulder gate) | 2 × 20 m/side | 2 × 10 m/side |
| 6 | Heavier bell at each carry; terminal: farmer ~50% BW per hand, suitcase ~50% BW, 40 m | 2 × 40 m | — |

Progress distance before load (20 → 30 → 40 m), then bell, then back to 20 m. Short on space:
1 m ≈ 1 s, so 40 m may be logged as 40 s.

## 5. Hardstyle standards and benchmarks

Coaching standards, not trial outcomes. Tested **every 8 weeks** like the `conditioning-hiit.md`
§7 benchmark, never as daily programming; a benchmark session replaces one accessory-rotation
KB session that week and is logged in the §8 benchmark table. While the scorecard in
`benchmarks.md` is active (added 2026-10-26), its due date is the clock and its table holds
the results; the protocol below is unchanged.

| Benchmark | Standard | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Simple** | 100 one-hand swings in 5:00 (10 every 30 s), 1 min rest, 10 get-ups in 10:00 (1/min, alternating); men 32 kg, women 24 kg swing / 16 kg get-up | StrongFirst, *Simple & Sinister* |
| **Sinister** | Same timing; men 48 kg; women 32 kg swing / 24 kg get-up | StrongFirst |
| **Snatch test** | 100 snatches in 5:00, free hand switches; bell by StrongFirst's sex/BW/age chart (24 kg men, 16 kg women common) | SFG I requirement |
| **Press** | Strict one-arm press of ~½ bodyweight, 1 rep/side | *Enter the Kettlebell* |
| **Carry** | Farmer carry ~50% BW per hand, 40 m unbroken | §4 Carry ladder terminal |

Rules: warm up as §6(a); one attempt per benchmark per session, at most two benchmarks (swing
and get-up count as one); film if possible; a form flag logs "attempt, form flag", not a pass;
the standard is met only at the standard bell.

## 6. Session templates

Durations per `output-format.md` ((sets × reps × tempo) + (sets − 1) × rest + 60 s per
exercise, warm-up at its own values, +15% margin). Tempo: swing 1.5 s/rep, grinds 3 s, cleans
2 s, get-up 35 s, carries 1 s/m.

### (a) KB-only full body (~30 min core)

Meets the `exercise-library.md` full-body rule. Suits **H blocks** (window top) and **S blocks**
(window floor, +1 set on A and E). Logged **per lift** as double-progression entries.

| Order | Exercise | Sets × reps | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warm-up | Halo 5/side, goblet squat 5 (light), 2H swing 10 (light) | 1 round | — |
| A | 2H Swing (hinge) | 3 × 15 | 60 s |
| B | Goblet Squat (squat, accessory slot) | 3 × 8 | 90 s |
| C | KB Floor Press (h_push) | 3 × 8/side | 60 s |
| D | Gorilla Row (h_pull) | 3 × 8/side | 60 s |
| E | 1-Arm Press (v_push) | 3 × 5/side | 90 s |
| F | Suitcase Carry (accessory) | 2 × 30 m/side | 60 s |

**31 min; 36 min with margin.** For a hard 30-min limit cut F (32 min), then B's third set.
Cut order is always F, then B, never A or E.

### (b) Swing density block (quick session)

- **EMOM 10 two-hand swings, 10 min** to start. Bell = the one at which 10 crisp swings take
  ≤ 20 s.
- +2 min per session (10 → 20). At 20 min clean: **up one bell, back to 10 min**. One-hand
  swings (5/5) may replace two-hand from Swing Step 4; same rule.
- Fail = a minute not completed or an ugly rep: hold the duration, repeat.
- **15-26 min** incl. warm-up and margin. Any block; deload at the previous bell and 10 min.
  Logged **as a unit** (minutes done / prescribed, bell); density score = reps × kg ÷ min.

### (c) Simple & Sinister session (Tsatsouline; coaching source)

- Warm-up 3 rounds: goblet squat 5, halo 5/side, glute bridge 5 (~5 min).
- **Swings 10 × 10 one-hand**, alternating hands each set; two-hand until Swing Step 4 is earned.
- **1 min rest**, then **get-ups 5/side**, alternating, 1/min when timed.
- **Timing tiers:** start untimed. When all 100 swings and 10 get-ups are crisp on **3
  consecutive sessions**, add the next target: 7:00 / 12:00 → 6:00 / 11:00 → **5:00 / 10:00**.
- **Bell step:** 5:00/10:00 met on 3 of 4 sessions → next bell by substitution: 2 of 10 swing
  sets and 1 get-up/side heavy, +2 sets and +1/side per session until all heavy; timing restarts
  untimed.
- **24 min** timed, ~30 untimed. Suits **deload weeks** (bell below current, untimed) and S
  blocks as the home hinge day. Logged as a unit: swing bell and time, get-up bell and time, tier.

### (d) Armor Building Complex (Dan John; coaching source)

- Double bells: **2 cleans, 1 press, 3 front squats**, unbroken, on the minute.
- Starting pair = the pair strict-pressed for 5. **EMOM 10** to start, +2 rounds per session to
  **20**, then next pair and back to 10. A round over 40 s of work or a pressed-out press = hold.
- Gates: Clean Step 4, Press Step 5, Front Squat Step 3, all earned first.
- **16-28 min.** Suits **H blocks**, not deload. Logged as a unit (rounds done / prescribed,
  pair); counts 1 set each toward quads, shoulders and hinge per 5 rounds (rules §11).

### (e) Clean & press ladders (Rite of Passage style; coaching source)

- Rung = clean + N presses left, then right; ladder = rungs 1-2-3. Rest between rungs ≈ the
  rung's duration, 2 min between ladders.
- Progression: 3 ladders of (1-2-3) → 4 → 5 → add a rung (1-2-3-4) at 3 ladders → 5 →
  (1-2-3-4-5) → next bell at 3 × (1-2-3).
- Pair with 2H swings 5 × 10 afterward on an S-block day.
- **21-32 min.** Suits **S blocks** (the press is the day's v_push). Logged **per lift** (press:
  bell, ladders × rungs) because it is a strength lift on a known bell.

### (f) Carry medley

- Round = farmer 40 m, rack 40 m/side, suitcase 40 m/side, waiter 20 m/side (waiter only past
  the shoulder gate, otherwise a second suitcase). 60 s between carries, 90 s between rounds,
  **3 rounds**.
- Progress distance, then bell, per the Carry ladder.
- **26 min.** Suits **deload weeks** and any block as the day's accessory; counts as a §2 bonus
  axial exposure in `bone-loading.md`. Logged per carry (bell, distance).

## 7. Where it slots in the rotation

- **Accessory pool.** Every §3 lift is a pool member tagged `KB`, entering, ageing and rotating
  out by the §6 cadence; `modality-rotation.md` governs how often the family is due.
- **Home substitute.** No DB or barbell at the location → the day type's main lift takes the KB
  equivalent **for that session only**, leaving the Implement Tier and e1RM thread untouched:
  h_push → floor press; v_push → 1-arm press; h_pull → gorilla row; hinge → KB deadlift or 2H
  swing (back flag permitting); squat → goblet or front squat. §2 calibration applies first time.
- **Deload variety.** S&S (§6c, untimed, one bell down) or a carry medley (§6f) is an allowed
  deload-week variety session (`modality-rotation.md` §2 rule 4). It covers a day type's deload
  only if its patterns cover that day type: S&S covers hinge and get-up but **not squat**, so it
  covers a legs day only with 2 × 5 goblet or front squats added at the deload bell (warm-up
  squats do not count); it never covers push or pull. Otherwise the day type is still owed its
  deload.
- **Quick sessions.** §6b and §6d are quick sessions; `quick-sessions.md` decides when one may
  replace a scheduled day.
- **Fatigue (mirrors `conditioning-hiit.md` §6).** Heavy swings (≥ Swing Step 6 bell, or any
  density block ≥ 15 min) never within 24 h before a leg day and never stacked before a lifting
  session. A §8 reactive-deload trigger cuts ballistics before grinds. A swing session on a
  HIIT day counts toward the HIIT weekly cap.
- **Volume.** Swings 1.0× glutes/hamstrings; cleans and snatches 1.0× hinge + 0.5× shoulders;
  carries count toward no muscle cap.

## 10. Safety

- **Room:** a clear 3 m lane front and back, a parking surface, never swing toward a person,
  mirror or glass.
- **Feet:** flat shoes or barefoot; the heel stays down.
- **Stop at the first ugly rep;** density blocks stop the minute form breaks and log the minutes.
- **Hands:** chalk, not gloves; bell in the fingers on ballistics; a tear ends ballistics for
  this session and the next, grinds continue.
- **Forearm bruising** on the clean or snatch = arc too wide: tame it, drop a bell, rebuild from
  the drill step. Never tough it out.
- **Overhead:** never behind the neck, never a bent-back wrist, never an unlocked elbow;
  lockout = biceps by the ear, ribs down.
- **Get-up:** eyes on the bell until standing; a wobble = park and reset, never twist to save it.
- **Floor:** park bells, never drop them; a swing is set down by hike-and-park.
- **Breathing:** sharp exhale on the snap/press; no prolonged holds in density work; blood-
  pressure concerns = breathe continuously.
- **Pain** of any kind → `injury-protocols.md`, regress one ladder step, §3 back-flag rule.
