# Kettlebell Repertoire (added 2026-10-18, user request)

Kettlebells ride on the SBS program in four ways: (a) home/anywhere load for the hinge,
squat, press, pull and carry patterns when the location has no dumbbells or barbell; (b) a
modality family (`KB` tag) for the Accessory Rotation (`sbs-progression-rules.md` §6) and the
modality-rotation rule (see `system/modality-rotation.md`); (c) the engine of several quick
sessions (see `system/quick-sessions.md`); (d) the home variant of HIIT Format B, already in
`conditioning-hiit.md` §3. A kettlebell lift **never replaces a main-lift tier** unless the
user has no other load at that location, and then only as the home substitute described in §7.
Per-user state lives in `users/<name>/training-state.md` under **## Kettlebell** (§8). Injury
gates in `users/<name>/injury-protocols.md` always win over this file.

## 1. The science, briefly (references in §11)

- **The swing builds maximal and explosive strength, at least in the short term.** Six weeks of
  twice-weekly swing training raised half-squat 1RM 9.8% and vertical jump 19.8%, matching
  jump-squat training (Lake & Lauder 2012a). Four weeks of swings matched explosive deadlifts
  for deadlift 1RM and jump height (Maulit 2017). Ten weeks of kettlebell work transferred to
  bench press and clean-and-jerk (Manocchia 2013). But when kettlebells were set against
  conventional weightlifting, the barbell group gained more strength; jump gains were similar
  (Otto 2012). A review of the field concluded the evidence supports kettlebells for **power**,
  and is **unclear for strength and aerobic fitness** (Beardsley & Contreras 2014).
- **Why the swing works: impulse.** A 32 kg swing produced a higher net impulse (~276 N·s) than
  a 60% 1RM back squat (~183 N·s) or a 40% jump squat (~231 N·s), with power comparable to the
  jump squat (Lake & Lauder 2012b). Swing demands rose with bell mass; velocity peaked at 16 kg.
  Hence the §2 rule that a swing is progressed by bell first and by reps second.
- **It is a hip-extension, hamstring-and-glute movement.** Swings activate the semitendinosus
  at 73-115% MVC, favouring it over biceps femoris (Zebis 2013). Glute activation reached ~80%
  MVC in McGill's model (below). The hardstyle cue is **"float, not lift"**: the hips throw the
  bell, the arms are ropes, the bell floats to chest height. A swing that is lifted with the
  shoulders stops being a hinge and loses the stimulus.
- **Spine load is real, and oddly shaped.** With a 16 kg bell, estimated L4/L5 compression
  reached ~3,200 N with back extensors ~50% MVC, and the shear was **posterior** (opposite in
  polarity to a conventional lift) (McGill & Marshall 2012). The authors note the pattern may
  not suit every back. That is why **every ballistic is gated by a back flag** in §3: deadlift
  and carries only while a back Pain Response Protocol is open.
- **Muscle demand differs across the ballistics.** Erector spinae were more active in the swing
  than the snatch; external obliques more in the snatch and clean than the swing; deltoid
  activity did not differ between swing, clean and snatch (Lyons 2017). The swing is the
  back-extensor-heaviest ballistic; the clean and snatch shift work to the trunk and arm.
- **Pain outcomes in workers.** 8 weeks of ballistic kettlebell work, 3x/week, cut neck/shoulder
  pain 2.1 points and low-back pain 1.4 points more than control and raised trunk-extensor
  strength (Jay 2011); the same cohort improved postural reactions to sudden perturbation
  (Jay 2013). Small (n = 40), one group, pain-reporting workers, not lifters.
- **Conditioning.** 12 min of continuous 16 kg swings sat at ~65% VO2max and ~87% HRmax
  (Farrar 2010). Four weeks of snatch intervals raised VO2max ~6% in collegiate athletes
  (Falatic 2015). A 12-min kettlebell Tabata produced higher oxygen uptake and energy cost than
  sprint-interval cycling, n = 8 (Williams & Kraemer 2015). So the swing/snatch is a legitimate
  HIIT Format B home variant, not a lifting substitute for the VO2max slot.
- **Older adults.** Hardstyle kettlebell training, 3 months, in insufficiently active 59-79
  year-olds was well tolerated and improved grip strength, 6-min walk, sit-to-stand and leg
  strength (Meigh 2022, BELL trial). The field's scoping review (99 papers) found the
  longitudinal evidence **largely underpowered and of low quality**, with only a weak
  recommendation for physical-function benefits (Meigh 2019).
- **Press.** At matched load, kettlebell and dumbbell overhead pressing produced no significant
  EMG difference, with kettlebell activity trending higher in most muscles (Blazkiewicz &
  Hadamus 2022). Treat the kettlebell press as a dumbbell press with an offset load, and the
  bottoms-up grip as a stability test rather than a strength builder.
- **Honest caveat.** Nearly every trial is 4-10 weeks, n = 8-40, novices or recreationally
  trained, several from one Danish group. There is no long-term RCT in trained men, no trial
  on the get-up or carries as isolated interventions, and the hardstyle benchmarks in §5 are
  coaching standards (StrongFirst, Tsatsouline, Dan John), not trial outcomes. The case for
  kettlebells here is practical: one implement loads five patterns anywhere, and the swing is
  the best-evidenced ballistic for a home hinge.

## 2. Bells and increments

| kg | 8 | 12 | 16 | 20 | 24 | 28 | 32 | 36 | 40 | 48 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| lb (approx) | 18 | 26 | 35 | 44 | 53 | 62 | 71 | 79 | 88 | 106 |

Log the bell in the unit stamped on it; the coaching view may show both.

**Why double progression needs a wider window.** §3 of the rules assumes ~5% load steps. A
bell step is 4 kg at best (16 to 20 kg = +25%, 24 to 32 kg with no 28 = +33%). A 6-10 window
cannot absorb that: the lifter tops the window, jumps a bell, and lands below the floor.

**The kettlebell double-progression rule:**
1. A kettlebell lift runs §3 double progression with an **extended window**: grinds (press,
   squat, row, deadlift, floor press, lunge) **8-15**; ballistics (swing, clean, snatch, high
   pull) **10-20**. Get-up and carries are not on reps: see their ladders (§4).
2. Advance = all sets at the window top, effort not "easy", **no form flag**. Next bell, reps
   reset to the window floor.
3. The **§3 forced-increment exception applies as written**: the first session at the new bell
   below the floor is not a failure. Hold and rebuild.
4. **No next bell.** Run the §5 Ceiling Test before any ceiling rung: (a) paired bells (two
   16s = 32 kg in the rack), (b) mixed DB + KB (a 20 kg bell and a 15 lb dumbbell for farmer
   carries), (c) unilateral conversion (two-hand swing to one-hand, goblet to single-rack).
   Only then the §5 ceiling ladder (Rung 1 extends the window to 15-25 for ballistics).
5. **Mixed-pair rule.** Unequal bells are allowed for double lifts only when the gap is one
   size; swap sides each set; log as `2 × 16/20 kg`. Never a mixed pair for the swing.

**Starting bell (the §2 "Unknown exercise" calibration gate).** First session only; adjust in
session, then normal rules apply.

| Lift | Men | Women | In-session check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Two-hand swing | 16 kg | 12 kg | 10 reps float to chest height with hip snap and no shoulder pull; if "easy" and crisp, +1 bell now |
| One-hand swing, clean, high pull | 16 kg | 12 kg | shoulders stay square; forearm lands soft on the clean |
| Snatch | 12-16 kg | 8-12 kg | lockout without a forearm bang, 5 clean reps/side |
| Press | 12-16 kg | 8 kg | 5 strict reps/side with ribs down |
| Goblet squat | 16 kg | 12 kg | full depth, heels down, 8 reps |
| Deadlift / suitcase deadlift | 24 kg | 16 kg | neutral spine, 8 reps |
| Get-up | shoe on fist | shoe on fist | §4 Get-up ladder; first bell 8-12 kg |
| Carries | ~25% BW per hand | ~25% BW per hand | 40 m without lean or grip loss |

### Prerequisite chain (hard gates, in one line each)

- **Hinge:** deadlift → two-hand swing → one-hand swing → clean → snatch. No step is skipped.
- **Squat:** goblet → single-rack front squat → double-rack front squat → racked lunge.
- **Press requires a clean** (the bell gets to the rack by a clean, never a curl or a swing-up).
- **Get-up is built in stages:** to elbow → to hand → to bridge/low sweep → to kneel → to stand,
  naked, then shoe on fist, then a light bell (§4).
- **Windmill requires a pain-free 30 s overhead hold** and a get-up to kneel. Bent press
  requires the windmill.
- **Bottoms-up press is the shoulder-stability diagnostic:** 5 clean reps/side at 12 kg (men)
  or 8 kg (women) clears the overhead carries.

### Flag routing (generic; the user's `injury-protocols.md` decides the specifics)

- **Shoulder flag:** snatch, overhead/waiter carry, windmill, bent press and bottoms-up carry
  are gated behind a pain-free get-up to kneel (Get-up ladder Step 4). Swings stay two-hand.
  Press runs half-kneeling until the same gate clears.
- **Knee flag:** racked lunges and full-depth front squats are gated exactly like the squat
  ladder in `bodyweight-ladders.md` (box depth before full depth, controlled tempo, regress one
  step on any sensation). Swings and deadlifts are unaffected (shins stay vertical).
- **Back flag:** while a back Pain Response Protocol is open, **no swings, cleans, snatches or
  high pulls**. Deadlift and carries only, at calibration loads, and only if pain-free. Closing
  the protocol reopens the ballistics at the ladder step **one below** where they were left.

## 4. Progression ladders

Ladder-mode rules from `bodyweight-ladders.md` apply (entry gate, stall limit 3 sessions, layoff
> 14 days = regress one step, pain = regress one step). "Crisp" = hip snap, bell floats, no
shoulder pull, no rounding, no grip failure. One ugly rep ends the set and the set does not
count toward the advance standard. Once a lift reaches its terminal step it leaves ladder mode
and runs §2 double progression; the ladder step is kept in state for regression.

### Swing ladder

| Step | Exercise | Advance At | Start At |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | KB deadlift, neutral spine, bell between the feet | 3 × 10 | 3 × 5 |
| 2 | Hike-pass and park (hike the bell back, stand, park it dead) | 3 × 5 clean hikes | 3 × 3 |
| 3 | Two-hand swing, chest height | 5 × 10 crisp | 3 × 10 |
| 4 | One-hand swing, shoulders square | 5 × 10/side crisp | 3 × 5/side |
| 5 | Hand-to-hand swing | 3 × 20 alternating | 3 × 10 |
| 6 | Heavier bell: return to Step 3 at the next bell (loop until no next bell) | Step 3 standard | 3 × 10 |
| 7 | Dead-stop swing at the heaviest two-hand bell (terminal, power) | 5 × 5 | 3 × 5 |

**Gap bridges:** towel swing (forces the float), band-around-the-bell swing, deadlift-to-swing
hybrid sets.

## 5. Hardstyle standards and benchmarks

Coaching standards, not trial outcomes. Benchmarked **every 8 weeks**, mirroring the
conditioning benchmark in `conditioning-hiit.md` §7, never as daily programming. A benchmark
session replaces one accessory-rotation KB session in that week and is logged in the
benchmark table (§8).

| Benchmark | Standard | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Simple** | 100 one-hand swings in 5:00 (10 every 30 s), 1 min rest, 10 get-ups in 10:00 (1/min, alternating); men 32 kg swing and get-up, women 24 kg swing / 16 kg get-up | StrongFirst, *Simple & Sinister* (Tsatsouline) |
| **Sinister** | Same timing; men 48 kg; women 32 kg swing / 24 kg get-up | StrongFirst |
| **Snatch test** | 100 snatches in 5:00, hand switches as needed; the test bell is set by sex, bodyweight and age on StrongFirst's chart; 24 kg (men) and 16 kg (women) are the common certification sizes | StrongFirst SFG I requirement |
| **Press** | Strict one-arm press of a bell ~½ bodyweight, 1 rep/side | Coaching benchmark (Tsatsouline, *Enter the Kettlebell*) |
| **Carry** | Farmer carry ~50% BW per hand for 40 m without setting down | Coaching benchmark (§4 Carry ladder terminal) |

**Benchmark rules.** Warm up as in §6(a). One attempt per benchmark per session; at most two
benchmarks in one session (swing/get-up count as one). Film if possible: a benchmark with a
form flag is logged "attempt, form flag", not as a pass. A bell between standards is logged at
the bell used; the standard is reached only at the standard bell.

## 6. Session templates

Durations are computed per `output-format.md`: (sets × reps × tempo) + (sets − 1) × rest +
60 s per exercise, warm-up at its own values, then +15% margin. Swing tempo 1.5 s/rep;
grinds 3 s/rep; cleans 2 s/rep; get-up 35 s/rep; carries 1 s/m.

### (a) KB-only full body (~30 min core)

Follows the exercise-library full-body rule (1 h_push, 1 h_pull, 1 squat or hinge, 1 v_push or
v_pull, 1-2 accessories). Suits **H blocks** (window top of each range) and **S blocks** (window
floor, +1 set on A and E). Logged **per lift** as double-progression entries.

| Order | Exercise | Sets × reps | Rest | Seconds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Warm-up | Halo 5/side, goblet squat 5 (light), 2H swing 10 (light) | 1 round | — | 180 |
| A | 2H Swing (hinge) | 3 × 15 | 60 s | 248 |
| B | Goblet Squat (squat, accessory slot) | 3 × 8 | 90 s | 312 |
| C | KB Floor Press (h_push) | 3 × 8/side | 60 s | 324 |
| D | Gorilla Row (h_pull) | 3 × 8/side | 60 s | 276 |
| E | 1-Arm Press (v_push) | 3 × 5/side | 90 s | 330 |
| F | Suitcase Carry (accessory) | 2 × 30 m/side | 60 s | 200 |

Total 1,870 s = **31 min**; with margin **36 min**. For a hard 30-min limit cut F (32 min with
margin), then drop B to 2 sets. Cut order is always F, then B's third set, never A or E.

### (b) Swing density block (quick session)

- **EMOM: 10 two-hand swings on the minute, 10 min** to start. Bell = the one at which 10
  crisp swings take ≤ 20 s (the rest of the minute is rest).
- **Progression:** add 2 min per session (10 → 12 → ... → 20). At 20 min clean, **go up one bell
  and back to 10 min**. One-hand swings (5/5) may replace two-hand at Swing Step 4+; same rule.
- Fail = any minute not completed or an ugly rep: hold the duration, repeat.
- Time: 10-20 min + 3 min warm-up, ×1.15 = **15-26 min**. Suits any block, deload at the
  previous bell and 10 min. Logged **as a unit**: minutes completed / prescribed, bell.
  Density score = total reps × bell kg ÷ minutes.

### (c) Simple & Sinister session (adapted from Tsatsouline; coaching source)

- Warm-up: 3 rounds of goblet squat 5, halo 5/side, glute bridge 5. ~5 min.
- **Swings: 10 × 10 one-hand**, alternating hands each set (5 L, 5 R). Two-hand at Swing
  Step 3 until Step 4 is earned.
- **1 min rest**, then **get-ups: 5/side**, alternating sides, 1 rep per minute when timed.
- **Timing tiers.** Start **untimed** (rest as needed). When all 100 swings and all 10 get-ups
  are completed crisp on **3 consecutive sessions**, add the next tier target:
  swings 7:00 / get-ups 12:00 → 6:00 / 11:00 → **5:00 / 10:00** (Simple timing).
- **Bell steps.** Meeting 5:00/10:00 on 3 of 4 sessions at a bell = go up one bell by
  substitution: session 1 at the new bell does 2 of the 10 swing sets and 1 get-up/side heavy,
  add 2 sets and 1 get-up/side per session until all are heavy, then timing restarts untimed.
- Time: timed version 16 min work + 5 warm-up, ×1.15 = **24 min**; untimed ~30 min. Suits
  **deload weeks** (at the bell below current, untimed) and S blocks as the home hinge day.
  Logged as a unit: swing bell and time, get-up bell and time, tier.

### (d) Armor Building Complex (Dan John; coaching source)

- Double bells: **2 cleans, 1 press, 3 front squats**, unbroken, on the minute.
- Starting pair = the pair the user can strict press for 5. **10 rounds EMOM** to start, +2
  rounds per session to **20**, then the next pair and back to 10. A round over 40 s of work or
  a pressed-out (non-strict) press = hold the round count.
- Gates: double clean (Clean Step 4), double press (Press Step 5), double front squat (Front
  Squat Step 3). All three must be earned first.
- Time: 10-20 min + 4 min warm-up, ×1.15 = **16-28 min**. Suits **H blocks**; not deload. Logged
  as a unit: rounds completed / prescribed, pair. Counts 1 set each toward quads, shoulders and
  hinge volume per 5 rounds (§11 of the rules).

### (e) Clean & press ladders (Rite of Passage style; coaching source)

- A rung = clean + N presses on the left, then the right. Ladder = rungs 1-2-3. Rest between
  rungs ≈ the rung's own duration; 2 min between ladders.
- **Progression:** 3 ladders of (1-2-3) → 4 → 5 ladders → add a rung (1-2-3-4) at 3 ladders →
  5 ladders → (1-2-3-4-5) → next bell at 3 × (1-2-3).
- Pair with 2H swings 5 × 10 after the ladders on an S-block day.
- Time: 3 ladders 14 min, 5 ladders 24 min, + 4 min warm-up, ×1.15 = **21-32 min**. Suits
  **S blocks** (the press is the day's v_push). Logged **per lift** (press: bell, ladders ×
  rungs) because it is a strength lift on a known bell.

### (f) Carry medley

- One round = farmer 40 m, rack 40 m/side, suitcase 40 m/side, waiter 20 m/side (waiter
  only past the shoulder gate; otherwise a second suitcase). 60 s between carries, 90 s between
  rounds. **3 rounds.**
- Progression: distance, then bell, per the Carry ladder.
- Time: 3 rounds ≈ 20 min + 3 min warm-up, ×1.15 = **26 min**. Suits **deload weeks** and any
  block as the day's accessory; counts as a §2 bonus axial exposure in `bone-loading.md`.
  Logged per carry (bell, distance).

## 7. Where it slots in the rotation

- **Accessory pool.** Every §3 lift is an Accessory Rotation pool member with modality tag
  `KB`. It enters, is kept, ages and rotates out by the §6 cadence like any accessory. The
  modality-rotation rule (`system/modality-rotation.md`) governs how often the `KB` family is
  due relative to DB, cable, band and bodyweight.
- **Home substitute.** When the location profile has no DB or barbell, the day type's main
  lift takes the KB equivalent **for that session only**, without touching the Implement Tier
  or the slot's e1RM thread: h_push → KB floor press; v_push → 1-arm press; h_pull → gorilla
  row; hinge → KB deadlift or 2H swing (back flag permitting); squat → goblet or front squat.
  Calibration rules of §2 apply the first time.
- **Deload variety.** An S&S session (§6c, untimed, one bell down) or a carry medley (§6f) is
  an allowed **deload-week variety session** at deload intensity (`modality-rotation.md` §2
  rule 4). It counts as a day type's deload coverage only if its patterns cover that day type:
  S&S covers the hinge and the get-up but **not the squat**, so it covers a legs day only with
  2 × 5 goblet or front squats added at the deload bell (the warm-up goblet squats do not
  count). It never covers push or pull. Otherwise it is a bonus session and the day type is
  still owed its deload.
- **Quick sessions.** Density blocks (§6b) and ABC (§6d) are quick sessions; `quick-sessions.md`
  decides when a quick session may replace a scheduled day.
- **Fatigue governance (mirrors `conditioning-hiit.md` §6).** Heavy swings (≥ Swing Step 6
  bell, or any density block ≥ 15 min) are **never within 24 h before a leg day**, and never
  stacked before any lifting session. A §8 reactive-deload trigger cuts KB ballistics before
  it cuts grinds. A swing session on the same day as HIIT counts toward the HIIT weekly cap.
- **Volume.** Swings count 1.0× toward glutes/hamstrings; cleans and snatches 1.0× hinge plus
  0.5× shoulders; carries do not count toward any muscle cap.

## 10. Safety

- **Room and path.** A clear 3 m lane front and back; nothing breakable in the swing arc; a
  surface the bell can be parked on. Never swing toward a person, a mirror or a glass door.
- **Flat shoes or barefoot.** No cushioned running shoes: the heel must stay down and feel the
  floor.
- **Stop at the first ugly rep.** A ballistic set ends at the first rep that rounds, pulls with
  the shoulders, or lands late. Never swing fatigued to form loss; density blocks stop the
  minute form breaks and log the minutes done.
- **Hands.** Chalk, not gloves. Handle the bell in the fingers, not the palm crease, on
  ballistics. File calluses weekly; a tear ends the ballistics for the session and the next,
  grinds continue. Rotate swing/clean/snatch volume across the week rather than stacking it.
- **Forearm.** Bruising on the clean or snatch means the arc is too wide: **tame the arc**
  (elbow in, bell close, punch through at the top), drop a bell, and rebuild from the
  ladder's drill step. Never "tough it out" through forearm contact.
- **Overhead.** Never behind the neck, never a press with the wrist bent back, never an
  overhead hold with the elbow unlocked. Lockout = biceps by the ear, ribs down.
- **Get-up.** Eyes on the bell until standing; a wobble means park it and reset, never save
  it with a twist. Practise naked and with a shoe before any bell.
- **Floor.** Park bells, do not drop them (feet, floors, and bells all crack). Setting down a
  swing is a hike-and-park, not a lower-through-the-front.
- **Breathing.** Hardstyle: sharp exhale on the snap/press, no prolonged breath-holds in
  density work; anyone with blood-pressure concerns breathes continuously.
- **Pain** of any kind: `injury-protocols.md`, regress one ladder step, and the back flag
  rule in §3 applies to every ballistic.
