# Quick Sessions — short programs for home or anywhere (added 2026-10-18, user request)

A catalogue of short sessions (4–20 min) that need little or no equipment: for days without a
full session, travel, "exercise snacks" between desk hours, and as scheduled phone reminders
(`system/reminders.md`). They are **bonus work**. Optional means: offered when the opener or a
reminder calls for one, never required, and skipping one is never a miss. A quick session is
**not a rotation day type**: it never closes a deload window, never advances a block, never
satisfies deload rotation coverage (`sbs-progression-rules.md` §6), and never replaces the HIIT
slot unless the user says so explicitly (then it is logged as HIIT only if it meets the
intensity in `conditioning-hiit.md` §3). Their value is adherence, movement frequency and the
dose-response evidence below, not program progress. State: `users/<name>/training-state.md`
under **Quick Sessions** (§5). Injury flags here are generic (shoulder / knee / back); the
user's `users/<name>/injury-protocols.md` always wins.

## 1. The science, briefly (references in §8)

- **The 7-Minute Workout is a practitioner article, not a trial.** Klika & Jordan (2013)
  proposed a 12-exercise bodyweight circuit, 30 s work / 10 s transition, repeated 2–3 times,
  in *ACSM's Health & Fitness Journal*. It synthesised circuit-training literature; it measured
  nothing. The one controlled trial of the protocol itself (Mattar 2017: 58 adults aged 18–30,
  daily for 6 weeks) found waist −4 cm and lower fat mass, and **no change in grip strength**.
- **Exercise snacks raise fitness.** The term comes from Francois (2014): 6 × 1 min incline
  walks before meals cut 24-h glucose in insulin-resistant adults. Jenkins (2019): 3 × 20 s
  vigorous stair climbs (60 steps), 1–4 h apart, 3 days/week for 6 weeks raised VO2peak in
  sedentary adults, modestly. Little (2019): three 20-s all-out sprints spread across the day
  matched the same sprints inside one 10-min session (+4% vs +6% VO2peak, no difference).
  Rafiei (2021): hourly 15–30 s stair bursts across 9 h of sitting lowered post-meal insulin
  16.5% in overweight adults and did nothing in lean young men. Islam (2022) reviews the
  field: proof-of-concept scale, promising, thin.
- **Vigorous bursts in daily life track with mortality.** In 25,241 non-exercisers wearing
  accelerometers, about 3 bouts/day of 1–2 min vigorous activity were associated with 38–40%
  lower all-cause and cancer mortality and 48–49% lower cardiovascular mortality (Stamatakis
  2022). Bouts of 1 to < 3 min carried a MACE hazard ratio of 0.71, 3 to < 5 min 0.62, 5 to
  < 10 min 0.59, and bouts under 1 min only helped when at least 15% vigorous (Ahmadi 2023).
  Observational, so association only, but it says **intensity and a few minutes beat nothing**.
- **Breaking up sitting works acutely.** Two-minute light or moderate walks every 20 min cut
  post-meal glucose and insulin in overweight adults (Dunstan 2012). In a dose-finding
  crossover, 5 min of walking every 30 min was the only dose that lowered glucose; every dose,
  down to 1 min per hour, lowered systolic blood pressure 4–5 mmHg (Duran 2023, n = 11, acute).
- **Accumulated bouts equal one continuous bout** for cardiorespiratory fitness and blood
  pressure across 19 studies (Murphy 2019). Splitting work up costs nothing measurable.
- **Minimal-dose lifting builds strength, suboptimally.** Trained men: one set of 6–12 reps at
  70–85% 1RM, 2–3×/week near failure, 8–12 weeks → significant but suboptimal squat and bench
  gains (Androulakis-Korakakis 2020). Time-efficient design: about 4 hard sets per muscle per
  week, 6–15 reps, bilateral multi-joint lifts, supersets to save clock (Iversen 2021). In the
  general and older population one set per exercise, once or twice a week, is meaningful (Fyfe
  2022; Nuzzo 2024 catalogues the strategies). Hypertrophy is dose-dependent: each added weekly
  set is worth about +0.37% (Schoenfeld 2017), which is why a quick session supplements and
  never substitutes.
- **Eccentric minimal dose.** One maximal 3-s eccentric elbow-flexor contraction a day, 5
  days/week for 4 weeks, raised strength 10–13% in untrained adults; isometric and concentric
  versions did far less and no muscle grew (Sato 2022). Six daily eccentrics beat 30 once a
  week: strength and thickness rose only in the daily group (Yoshida 2022). At tiny doses,
  **frequency matters more than per-session volume**. Novice data; expect less when trained.
- **"Tabata" is a clock, not a protocol.** Tabata (1996) was 7–8 × 20 s at ~170% VO2max with
  10 s rests on a cycle ergometer in trained young men, and it raised VO2max and anaerobic
  capacity. A bodyweight circuit on that clock cannot reach that intensity; most "Tabata"
  workouts are 4-min circuits, fine as long as nobody expects Tabata's result.
- **What the evidence does NOT support:** that a 7-minute circuit matches a full program for
  strength or hypertrophy. Time-capped bodyweight sets never reach the §6 RIR targets for the
  big movers, and no trial shows max-strength gains from it in trained people. The 10-min
  sprint session (Gillen 2016) is already HIIT Format C in `conditioning-hiit.md`.

## 2. Rules of engagement

**(a) When to offer.** Offer a quick session when the opener contains a time budget under
about 25 min, or any of: "snack", "quick", "travel", "hotel", "no equipment", "7-minute",
"desk"; or when a reminder from `system/reminders.md` has fired. A budget of 25–35 min is a
shortened normal session (operating-protocol §2 time-limit cut), not a quick session, unless
asked for by name. Never offer one unprompted on a day already trained (walk-break and
mobility excepted).

**(b) Fatigue governance** (same priority order as `conditioning-hiit.md` §6: bonus work is
cut first, lifting last):
- No lower-body-heavy entry (Legs = L in the §4 table) within 24 h before a scheduled legs day.
- On a deload week, only the **mobility** and **core-light** families (and walk-breaks); nothing
  vigorous. A quick session never counts as a deload session and never closes the window.
- Skip entirely for 48 h after a §8 reactive-deload trigger fires or a Pain Response Protocol
  opens on a joint the entry loads. Entries that avoid the joint completely may still run
  (walk-break with a shoulder event; mobility with a knee event, minus ankle and squat items).
- Any mention of pain routes to `users/<name>/injury-protocols.md` and ends the quick session.

**(c) Weekly cap.** Vigorous entries (Vig = yes in §4) count toward the **3 cardio sessions
per 7 days** cap in `conditioning-hiit.md` §2, alongside HIIT. Reminder-fired stair snacks
count one per day, not one per bout. Walk-breaks, mobility and GtG never count.

**(d) Volume accounting.** Sets from quick sessions count toward §11 weekly volume at **0.5×**
(they are not taken to the prescribed RIR) unless the user reports them "hard", then 1.0×.
The 20-minute full body (§3.11) counts 1.0×: it is a real lifting session. Nothing here is
impact, so nothing feeds the bone tally.

**(e) Progression stays inside the quick session.** Each entry has its own small ladder:
**rounds first, then work:rest, then variation**. Advance one step when the current step is
completed with form intact twice in a row. Never edit a main lift's state, e1RM, rep window
or ladder step from a quick session; the only exceptions are §3.10 (GtG) and §3.11.

**(f) Scaling.** Every entry carries an easier / standard / harder line. Serve **standard**
unless state says otherwise; serve **easier** on the first exposure to an entry, after any
flag swap, and when the last report said "cut short".

## 3. The catalogue

Technique and bell selection for every kettlebell entry live in `system/kettlebell.md`;
every TRX entry in `system/suspension-trainer.md`; bodyweight steps in
`system/bodyweight-ladders.md`. Nothing below re-states their ladders.

### 3.1 7-Minute Workout (Klika & Jordan 2013)
**Time** 7 min per circuit, 1–3 circuits · **Equipment** chair, wall · **Suits** anyone,
anywhere; the default "quick" when no family is named.

| # | Exercise (published order) | Work | Transition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jumping jacks | 30 s | 10 s |
| 2 | Wall sit | 30 s | 10 s |
| 3 | Push-up | 30 s | 10 s |
| 4 | Abdominal crunch | 30 s | 10 s |
| 5 | Step-up onto chair | 30 s | 10 s |
| 6 | Squat | 30 s | 10 s |
| 7 | Triceps dip on chair | 30 s | 10 s |
| 8 | Plank | 30 s | 10 s |
| 9 | High knees, running in place | 30 s | 10 s |
| 10 | Lunge | 30 s | 10 s |
| 11 | Push-up and rotation | 30 s | 10 s |
| 12 | Side plank (15 s/side) | 30 s | — |

Rest 60 s between circuits. Pace each 30 s to finish with 2–3 reps in reserve, not to a max.
**Scaling** easier: 1 circuit, 20 s / 20 s · standard: 2 circuits, 30 s / 10 s · harder: 3
circuits, then §3.2. **Ladder**: circuits 1 → 2 → 3, then work:rest 30:10 → 40:10, then §3.2.

| Flag | Swap |
|---|---|
| Knee | Wall sit → glute bridge; jumping jacks → step-jacks (no hop); step-up height ≤ 8 in (a stair, not a chair); lunge → reverse lunge, short stride; high knees → marching in place |
| Shoulder | Triceps dip → band pull-apart or TRX row (`suspension-trainer.md`); push-up and rotation → push-up, no rotation; side plank → side plank from knees, or dead bug if the forearm hurts |
| Back | Abdominal crunch → McGill curl-up; plank → hardstyle plank 2 × 10 s; lunge → split squat, torso upright; side plank from knees |

**Logs** to the Quick Sessions log only (0.5× volume). **Reminder-friendly?** Yes ("strength
snack"; the trainer picks the circuit count).

### 3.2 7-Minute Advanced (this repo's own escalation; no ACSM "advanced" paper exists)
Same clock and transitions as 3.1, 2–3 circuits, harder movements from the ladders:
1 jump squat (knee flag: squat ladder step) · 2 decline push-up · 3 hollow hold · 4 step-up
with knee drive · 5 reverse lunge · 6 dip, current ladder step · 7 hardstyle plank · 8 mountain
climbers · 9 pike push-up · 10 single-leg glute bridge (15 s/side) · 11 push-up and rotation ·
12 side plank with top-leg lift. **Scaling** easier: 2 circuits with 3.1 movements for items
1, 2, 6 · standard: 2 circuits · harder: 3 circuits, 40 s / 10 s. Flags: as 3.1; shoulder flag
replaces items 6 and 9 with inverted row and band pull-apart. **Legs** L.
**Reminder-friendly?** No; serve from an opener.

### 3.3 Tabata-style 4-minute (8 × 20 s / 10 s, one movement)
**Equipment** none, or a kettlebell or bike · **Suits** a vigorous hit in under 6 min.
Pick **one** movement, 8 rounds of 20 s hard / 10 s rest, burpee-free options only:
air squat (standard) · KB two-hand swing (`kettlebell.md`; Legs L) · bike (RPE 17–18, the
only option near Tabata's intensity) · step-jacks (knee flag) · mountain climbers (not with a
shoulder flag). **Honesty note**, printed the first time: "This is Tabata's clock, not
Tabata's intensity; it counts as a vigorous snack, not a HIIT session." **Scaling** easier:
6 rounds, 15 s / 15 s · standard: 8 × 20 / 10 · harder: two 4-min blocks, 2 min between,
different movements. **Ladder**: rounds, then work:rest, then a second block. **Vig** yes.
**Reminder-friendly?** Yes.

### 3.4 10-minute core circuit
**Equipment** floor; a band for Pallof press (else bird dog) · **Suits** rest days, deload
weeks (the "core-light" version), travel.

| Role | Standard movement | Work | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anti-extension | Dead bug (or hardstyle plank) | 40 s | 20 s |
| Anti-rotation | Pallof press, band (no band: bird dog) | 40 s (20 s/side) | 20 s |
| Anti-lateral flexion | Side plank | 40 s (20 s/side) | 20 s |
| Hip flexion | Reverse crunch (or lying leg raise) | 40 s | 20 s |

Two rounds, 60 s between rounds; 9 min with the warm-up.
**Back flag, or "core-light" on a deload week: McGill Big 3** (McGill 2010; *Low Back
Disorders*; *Back Mechanic*). Curl-up, side plank (each side), bird dog (each side), each in
the **descending pyramid 5-3-1**: 5 reps of a 10-s hold, 30 s rest, 3 reps, 30 s rest, 1 rep.
No spinal flexion under load, no crunches, no leg raises. About 10 min.
**Scaling** easier: one round, or pyramid 3-2-1 · standard: as above · harder: three rounds,
or holds of 10 s with the pyramid 6-4-2. **Ladder**: rounds, then hold length, then variation
(dead bug → hardstyle plank → ab-wheel if present). **Legs** 0. **Reminder-friendly?** Yes.

### 3.5 Kettlebell snacks (technique, bell choice and gates: `system/kettlebell.md`)
All four are **Legs L** and vigorous except the carry medley (Vig no, Legs m).

| Entry | Time | Prescription |
|---|---|---|
| Swing EMOM 10 | 10 min | 10 two-hand swings on each minute × 10 min (100 swings); rest the remainder of each minute |
| S&S-lite | 10–12 min | 5 × 10 two-hand swings on the minute, then 3 get-ups/side, alternating sides, ~1 rep every 45 s |
| ABC 10-min | 10 min | Armor Building Complex: 2 double cleans + 1 double press + 3 double front squats, on each minute × 10 (single bell: do the complex per side, one side per minute) |
| Carry medley | 8–10 min | Farmer carry 40 m → rack carry 40 m → suitcase carry 40 m/side, 60 s rest, 3 rounds |

**Scaling** easier: EMOM 6, S&S-lite 3 × 10 + 2 get-ups/side, ABC every 90 s, medley 2 rounds
· standard: as table · harder: EMOM 15 (still 10 swings/min), S&S-lite 8 × 10 + 4 get-ups, ABC
12 min, medley 4 rounds. **Ladder**: minutes/rounds, then the bell step. Shoulder flag: no
get-ups, no presses (ABC becomes clean + front squat); back flag: no swings until
`injury-protocols.md` clears the hinge. **Reminder-friendly?** Swing EMOM 10 yes; others no.

### 3.6 TRX 10-minute circuit (steps and setup: `system/suspension-trainer.md`)
Row → press → hamstring curl → fallout → split squat (20 s/side), **40 s work / 20 s rest,
2 rounds**, 60 s between rounds. Strap angle sets the load; use the current TRX ladder step.
**Scaling** easier: 30 s / 30 s, 2 rounds · standard: as above · harder: 3 rounds, or 45 s /
15 s. **Ladder**: rounds, then work:rest, then strap angle/step. Shoulder flag: TRX plank in
place of the fallout, press at a high angle only; knee flag: split squat → TRX-assisted squat.
**Legs** m · **Vig** yes. **Reminder-friendly?** Yes, where a TRX is anchored at home.

### 3.7 Stair snack (Jenkins 2019 protocol)
**Equipment** three flights (about 60 steps) · **Suits** office or apartment days.
Per bout: 30–60 s warm-up (10 jumping jacks, 10 air squats, 5 lunges/leg; step-jacks with a
knee flag), then **one vigorous climb of ~60 steps in ~20 s**, walk down slowly as the
cool-down. **3 bouts, separated by 1–4 h**, up to 3 days/week; a day's three bouts count as
**one** vigorous session toward the cap. **Scaling** easier: 2 bouts, one flight at a time ·
standard: 3 × 60 steps · harder: 3 × 2 climbs with 60 s walk between, or a loaded backpack.
Knee flag: climb only, never run down; stop at any front-knee comment. **Legs** L · **Vig**
yes. **Reminder-friendly?** Yes: the best reminder target in the file.

### 3.8 Walk-break snack (Duran 2023; Dunstan 2012)
**5 min brisk walk every 30 min of sitting** (the dose that lowered glucose); fallback **2 min
every 20 min** (Dunstan) or **1 min every 60 min** (blood pressure only). Indoors is fine; a
staircase is a bonus, not a requirement. Not vigorous, not capped, no volume, no flag swaps
beyond the user's walking gates. **Legs** 0. **Reminder-friendly?** Yes; carry a sitting
window (start and end of desk hours) in `reminders.md`, not a fixed count.

### 3.9 Mobility / prehab 5-minute
**If the user has a prehab block in `training-state.md`, that block IS the mobility snack.
Never invent a second one.** Otherwise: half-kneeling hip flexor stretch 45 s/side · open book
8/side (thoracic rotation) · knee-to-wall ankle rocks 10/side · shoulder CARs 3/side, slow ·
hip CARs 3/side. One round, no rest, about 5 min. **Scaling** easier: 30 s holds and 2 CARs ·
standard · harder: two rounds. Nothing is loaded, so the only flag rule is: an item that
reproduces a pain is deleted. **Legs** 0. **Reminder-friendly?** Yes; the natural morning one.

### 3.10 Grease-the-groove snack (bodyweight-ladders gap bridge #1)
Pull-up or push-up, **submaximal sets through the day**: each set **≤ 50% of the tested max at
the current ladder step**, sets **≥ 1 h apart**, **stop the day at the first slow rep**, 3–6
sets/day, 3–5 days/week. Never in the 24 h before the lifting day that tests that pattern (pull
day for pull-ups, push day for push-ups). **Scaling** easier: 3 sets at 40% · standard · harder:
6 sets at 50%, or one step up with the same cap. **Ladder**: sets/day, then percentage, then
step. The daily total is the only thing that progresses; it reports to the bodyweight ladder
as bridge work and the step is re-tested in a normal session after 1–2 weeks. Shoulder flag:
pain-free step only; no GtG pull-ups with an open shoulder event. **Legs** 0 · **Vig** no.
**Reminder-friendly?** Yes, as an hourly nudge carrying the per-set rep count.

### 3.11 20-minute full body (3 sets) — when "quick" means a real but short lift
Squat-or-hinge, push, pull in that order, **3 × 8–12 each, 60 s rest**, from whatever is
present: DB or KB (goblet squat, KB deadlift, DB floor press, DB row), TRX (row, press,
hamstring curl) or a bodyweight ladder step; 2 min of easy movement first. Computed duration
≈ 18 min with the 15% margin (output-format Block 1 rule).
**The boundary:** if an exercise is one of the user's tracked lifts, its sets **log to that
lift's normal state** as a shortened session: loads from state, reps 1.0× toward §11, decision
tree applied with one adjustment. Because the rest is 60 s rather than the §12 value, a
shortfall of ≤ 2 reps against target is a note, not a failure, and feeds neither plateau
detection (§7) nor a reactive deload (§8); an overshoot is real and §13a applies. Untracked
exercises log to the Quick Sessions log only. **It never advances the rotation** unless the
user says "count it as today's [day]"; then it is logged as that day type, marked "shortened",
and the rotation moves. **Scaling** easier: 2 sets · standard: 3 × 8–12 · harder: add one
accessory (now a 25–30 min normal session). **Legs** L · **Vig** no. **Reminder-friendly?** No.

### 3.12 Hotel / no-equipment 15-minute full body ladder
Push-up (current ladder step) → squat (current ladder step) → row (inverted row under a sturdy
desk; else a knotted towel round a closed door's handle; else backpack row) → plank (current
core-ladder step), **40 s work / 20 s rest, 3 rounds**, 60 s between rounds; about 13 min plus
a 2-min warm-up. Steady pace, stopping 2–3 reps short of failure each interval, never a max. **Scaling** easier: 2 rounds, 30 s / 30 s, one step down
· standard · harder: 4 rounds, or push-up and squat one step up. **Ladder**: rounds, then
work:rest, then step. Flags: shoulder → row at a higher angle and push-up at the pain-free
step; knee → squat ladder step 1–2 with a controlled tempo; back → plank becomes the McGill
curl-up and bird dog. **Legs** m · **Vig** no. **Reminder-friendly?** No (travel opener).

## 4. Rotation of quick sessions

- Never serve the same entry more than **2 times in a row**; the third serving in a run comes
  from a different entry, preferably a different family.
- Rotate **families week to week**: cardio snacks this week → strength or core offered first
  next week, unless the opener names one.
- **Reminders carry the family; the trainer picks the instance** at serve time from state,
  flags, the 24-h legs rule and the weekly cap. A reminder never names a fixed entry.
- Walk-break and mobility sit outside the rotation and may be served any day.

| Family | Entries | Legs | Vig |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cardio | 3.3 Tabata-style · 3.7 Stair snack · 3.5 Swing EMOM 10 | L | yes |
| Strength | 3.1 7-Min · 3.2 7-Min Advanced · 3.5 S&S-lite, ABC, carry medley · 3.6 TRX-10 · 3.10 GtG · 3.11 Full-20 · 3.12 Hotel-15 | see entry | 3.1/3.2/3.6 yes; others no |
| Core | 3.4 Core-10 (standard) · 3.4 McGill Big 3 ("core-light") | 0 | no |
| Mobility | 3.9 Mobility-5 (or the user's prehab block) · 3.8 Walk-break | 0 | no |

Legs: L = lower-body-heavy (24-h rule before legs day), m = mixed (rule applies only if legs
is tomorrow), 0 = none. Vig = counts toward the weekly cardio cap.

**Reporting**, parsed by `reporting-protocol.md`: **unchanged line = done**; **a number** on a

opens the Pain Response Protocol. **Reminder replies**: "did my [name]" = full completion at
the current ladder position; "did half" or a number = that fraction; silence = not done, no
nudge beyond the next scheduled reminder. A quick session never produces a `RESULTS PENDING`
entry in `workout-log.md`; it is logged only in the Quick Sessions log, and only when reported.

## 7. Safety

- **Warm-up minimum: 60 s of easy movement**, even before a 4-min session; stair and sprint
  snacks use the Jenkins warm-up in §3.7. Never go from a chair straight to all-out.
- **Vigorous entries need the HIIT screening question** (`conditioning-hiit.md` §8: chest pain
  or pressure, fainting, unusual breathlessness, palpitations), asked **once**, dated in
  `screeningAnswered`; one answer covers both files.
- **Stop rules**: stop the session for any of the symptoms above (and see a doctor), for any
  joint pain (Pain Response Protocol), for form breaking on a loaded movement (swing, clean,
  get-up), or when the user says stop. Finish a vigorous snack with 60 s of walking.
- **First two weeks of any vigorous entry run at the easier scaling**, RPE ≤ 17; nothing
  all-out until the HIIT entry doses have been met once. No stimulant or heavy pre-workout
  before a vigorous snack; nothing but mobility or a walk-break inside 2 h of bedtime.
