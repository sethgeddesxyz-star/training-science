# Suspension Trainer (TRX) Repertoire (added 2026-10-18)

Straps (TRX-style; gymnastic rings count) are (a) the most complete "anywhere" kit the program
has, covering every pattern except a heavy hinge; (b) the `TRX` modality family for the
Accessory Rotation (`sbs-progression-rules.md` §6, `modality-rotation.md`); (c) the home/travel
substitute for the Tier-1 row and press slots; (d) core co-contraction and single-leg work
machines do not give; (e) building blocks for `quick-sessions.md`. **Never a main-lift tier for
push or pull** unless the user has no other load. Strap exercises run in **ladder mode**
(`bodyweight-ladders.md` general rules) where a step is a (mark, variation) pair (§2). Per-user
state: `users/<name>/training-state.md` **## Suspension Trainer** (§7). Flags here are generic;
the user's `injury-protocols.md` always wins.

---

## 1. The science, briefly (references in §10)

- **Straps add instability and core demand, not load.** A suspended push-up raises pec, delt
  and triceps EMG and lifts rectus abdominis from ~21% to ~68% MVIC (Snarr & Esco 2013a, c);
  across four systems the abs are highly active while pecs and delts are better served by stable
  set-ups (Calatayud 2014). **A strap press is a push-plus-plank**, not a heavier push.
- **Rows are the exception.** The suspended row matches the fixed-bar row (Snarr & Esco 2013b);
  the 18-study review finds suspension versions out-activate their twins **except the inverted
  row** (Aguilera-Castells 2020). The TRX row is a fair row.
- **Core work on straps is genuinely hard.** Suspended planks, hip abduction in plank, hamstring
  curl, rollout, body saw and pike all drive trunk muscles to ~55-60% MVIC or above (Byrne 2014;
  Mok 2015; Fong 2015; Cugliari & Boccia 2017; Snarr 2016). Suspended pushes carry more spine
  compression than stable ones (McGill 2014): hence the §3 back-flag rules.
- **Load is a function of angle and strap length.** Strap tension rises toward vertical, ~50-75%
  BW at the steepest setting (Gulmez 2017); strap length is a progression variable (Giancotti
  2018); resistance by angle and distance was mapped by Melrose & Dawes (2015). Basis of the §2
  mark scale.
- **Training trials show parity, not superiority.** TRX circuits matched stable circuits in
  untrained men (Maté-Muñoz 2014) and adults (Janot 2013); older adults complied at 85% (Gaedtke
  & Morat 2015) and gained grip strength and mass vs band work (Campa 2021). A 60-min TRX
  workout averaged ~69% HRmax: moderate conditioning (Dudgeon 2015).
- **The limit.** Instability cuts force, power and velocity, which is why free weights stay the
  foundation (Behm 2010). Most evidence is acute EMG; **nothing shows straps beat loaded training
  for hypertrophy or max strength.** Their defensible value: core co-contraction, finely scalable
  unilateral work, joint-friendly pulling, portability.

## 2. How load works on straps: the angle rule

Resistance is a fraction of bodyweight set by **body angle**, which is set by **where the feet
stand relative to the anchor line** (the floor point under the anchor). Rows and presses alike:
**feet closer to or past the anchor line = steeper = heavier.** Elbow flexion adds load on top,
so a short rep is a missed rep.

### Foot-position marks 0-8

| Mark | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Body angle | ~70° | ~65° | ~60° | ~52° | ~45° | ~38° | ~30° | ~20° | ~horizontal |
| Load, % BW | ~20 | ~27 | ~33 | ~40 | ~47 | ~54 | ~60 | ~67 | ~75 |

Mark 0 = feet far from the anchor line, one half-step closer per mark, Mark 7 = at the line,
Mark 8 = past it or feet on a box. Percentages order the steps only, never an e1RM input (§6).

**Calibration (once per anchor, per strap length, per family: row, press).** Tape Mark 0 at ~70°
and Mark 8 at ~horizontal, then 7 marks at equal spacing. Record `marks calibrated: y, <date>`
(§7). A new anchor or strap-length change for that family = recalibrate before the next
prescription. Without tape the ladders below are guesswork.

### Strap length settings

| Setting | Used for |
|---|---|
| Fully lengthened | Chest press, chest fly, tricep extension, assisted squat / pistol, Y/T/W, overhead reach |
| Mid-length | Row (all grips), face pull, high row, bicep curl, fallout, Pallof press, thoracic rotation |
| Mid-calf (handles at mid-calf) | Hamstring curl, hip press, any feet-in-cradles move, atomic push-up, suspended Bulgarian |
| Over-shortened (handles at chest under the anchor) | Single-arm row, single-arm press, archer row, rear-delt fly |

### Ladder mode on straps (`bodyweight-ladders.md` general rules apply unchanged)

- **Step = (mark, variation).** **Advance** at 3 × 8 clean (3 × 30 s holds) → next mark at
  3 × 5. **Marks first, variations after:** Mark 8 at 3 × 8 → the next §4 variation at the mark
  its row names, never Mark 8 of the new variation.
- Entry gate, stall limit (3 sessions → gap bridge), pain rule and deload (65% volume) as
  written there. Strap gap bridges: 3-s eccentric at the harder mark; hybrid set (first 5 reps
  harder mark, rest current); partial ROM at the harder mark.
- **Missed time** (`sbs-progression-rules.md` §9 ladder mode): regress **1 mark per 14 full
  days**, floor Mark 0, restart at 3 × 5. Layoff never drops a variation; only the entry gate does.
- A mark advance resets `stallCounter`; no advance at effort "hard"/"failed" increments it.

## 4. Ladders (format and general rules of `bodyweight-ladders.md`)

Steps 1-9 of a marked ladder are Marks 0-8 of the base variation (step = mark + 1). Variations
begin at the mark the row names. Unilateral steps use `/side`. First exposure to any new
variation is at the start target, never the advance target.

### Ladder T1: TRX Row (h_pull)

| Step | Exercise | Advance At | Start At |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-9 | TRX Row, neutral grip, Marks 0-8 | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 10-14 | TRX Single-arm Row, Marks 2-6 | 3 × 8/side | 3 × 5/side |
| 15 | TRX Row, feet elevated 12-18", Mark 8 | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 16 | TRX Archer Row, Mark 6 (terminal; Marks 7-8 after) | Build reps | 3 × 4/side |

Gap bridges: 3-s eccentric at the next mark; hybrid set; one session underhand at the next mark.

### Ladder T2: TRX Press (h_push)

| Step | Exercise | Advance At | Start At |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-9 | TRX Chest Press, Marks 0-8 | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 10 | TRX Chest Press, feet elevated 12-18", Mark 8 | 3 × 8 | 3 × 5 |
| 11-14 | TRX Single-arm Press, Marks 2-5 | 3 × 8/side | 3 × 5/side |
| 15 | Push-up, feet in straps | 3 × 10 | 3 × 6 |
| 16 | TRX Atomic Push-up (terminal) | Build reps | 3 × 5 |

Gap bridges: 3-s eccentric or partial ROM at the next mark; hybrid set. Shoulder flag: steps
11-14 need a pain-free step 9 on 2 consecutive sessions.

### Ladder T3: TRX Fallout / Rollout (core, anti-extension)

| Step | Exercise | Advance At | Start At |
|---|---|---|---|

Gap bridges: reduced reach at the next mark; 3-s eccentric; hybrid set. Back flag: whole ladder

## 5. Session templates

Durations per `output-format.md` ((sets × reps × tempo) + (sets − 1) × rest + 60 s per
exercise, +15% margin); tempo 3 s/rep, 4 s for single-leg squats. Marks and steps come from the
user's state; templates fix slots, sets, reps and rest only. The general warm-up (3-5 min easy
movement, plus the I-Y-T set under a shoulder flag) is a WARMUP block inside the quoted total.
Sets count toward weekly volume at the usual 1.0× / 0.5× rates (see §6 for the mark condition).

### (a) TRX Full Body (~35 min with margin; meets the `exercise-library.md` full-body rule)

| # | Slot | Exercise | Sets × reps | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | h_pull | TRX Row (ladder T1 step) | 3 × 8 | 90 s |
| 2 | h_push | TRX Chest Press (ladder T2 step) | 3 × 8 | 90 s |
| 3 | squat | TRX Suspended Bulgarian (Assisted Squat if gated) | 3 × 8/side | 90 s |
| 4 | hinge | TRX Hamstring Curl (ladder T4 step) | 3 × 10 | 60 s |

| 6 | core | TRX Fallout (ladder T3 step; Side Plank if back flag) | 3 × 8 | 60 s |

~30 min core, **35 min with margin**. Over the user's limit → cut from #6 upward.

|---|---|---|---|---|

### (c) TRX Lower + Core (~36 min with margin)

| # | Slot | Exercise | Sets × reps | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | squat | TRX Assisted Single-leg Squat (ladder T5 step) | 3 × 6/side | 90 s |
| 2 | squat | TRX Suspended Bulgarian | 3 × 8/side | 90 s |
| 3 | hinge | TRX Hamstring Curl (ladder T4 step) | 3 × 10 | 60 s |
| 4 | hinge | TRX Hip Press | 3 × 12 | 60 s |
| 5 | core | TRX Body Saw (TRX Pallof Press if back flag) | 3 × 8 | 60 s |
| 6 | core | TRX Side Plank, feet in cradles | 3 × 20 s/side | 60 s |

~31 min core, **36 min with margin**.

### (d) 10-min TRX circuit (detail in `quick-sessions.md`)

Six moves, **30 s work / 20 s change, 2 rounds = 10 min**: Row (Mark 3) → Chest Press (Mark 3)
→ Assisted Squat → Hamstring Curl → Fallout (kneeling if unsure) → Mountain Climber. Logged as
a quick session; ladder state does not change.

### (e) Travel-week rule

Away **≥ 5 days with straps only**: run **TRX Full Body (a)** on the normal rotation cadence in
place of each lifting day type that comes due (HIIT runs its home variants). Ladder entries
progress normally. **The block week does not advance** in travel mode, as home-only sessions
substitute the Tier-1 member without advancing the tier; on return the block resumes at the week
it left. Set `travel mode: y` at the first travel session, clear it at the first session back,
and run the §9 missed-time check on the loaded lifts not trained.

**Cap on the hold.** The §8 hard cap (never more than 6 loading weeks without a deload) is a
calendar rule, so travel mode may hold the week for **at most 14 days**. A longer trip ends the
block where it stands; the first week back is the deload (65% of pre-trip loads, after the §9
missed-time table), then the next block opens.

## 6. Where it slots in the rotation

- **Accessory pool members** tagged `TRX` are eligible for any accessory slot at a location
  whose `equipment-profiles.md` entry lists an anchor.
- **Ceiling ladder Rung 3** (`sbs-progression-rules.md` §5): a DB/machine exercise at its
  ceiling may swap to the unilateral strap version (single-arm row or press, single-leg curl,
  assisted pistol). The slot enters ladder mode at the mark the §4 ladder names and leaves it
  only by the user's choice at a block boundary.
- **Accessory Rotation replacement candidate** (§6 "choosing the replacement"): a `TRX` member
  meets rule 1 against a DB, cable or machine incumbent on position bias (hardest at the
  stretched bottom) and implement change; rules 2-6 still apply. Rotated in as an accessory it
  carries the accessory cadence (1-block hold, due at 2 boundaries) though it is a ladder; its
  ladder state persists in §7 so it resumes where it left.
- **Deload-week variety session:** allowed, at the ladder deload (65% volume, same mark).
- **Travel sessions:** §5(e).
- **What it does NOT do:** no main-lift tier progress (a strap row or press in a Tier-1 slot is
  a substitution); no e1RM from a mark; no Strength-wave entry (in a Strength block a TRX
  exercise runs its ladder as written); no AMRAP sets.
- **Volume counting.** Ladder mode has no RIR target, so a low-mark set may be far from failure.
  A strap set counts toward §11 weekly volume at the usual 1.0× / 0.5× rates at **Mark 3 or
  higher**, or at any mark when the user reports it hard or it ends within 2 reps of the limit;
  otherwise at half those rates. Circuit-style strap work in a quick session follows
  `quick-sessions.md` §2.

## 8. Output

**Coaching view:** standard header and Working Sets format; the load field carries the mark or
variation, the progression note names the ladder and step:

```
3. **TRX Row** — 3 x 8 @ Mark 4 (neutral) | Rest: 90s
   > Progression note: Ladder T1 step 5; 3 × 8 clean at Mark 3 → advanced, reps reset to 5 (or: holding Mark 4, 7/8/6 last time)
   > Last session: Mark 3 — 8/8/8
```

**Checklist:** per `output-format.md` (one line per set, rest line once above the exercise,
blank line between exercises). Load field = mark, or the variation when the lever is not a mark;
unilateral stays on one line with `/side`:

```
WORKING
TRX Row — R1m30s
TRX Row — Set 1 — Mark 4 — 10
TRX Row — Set 2 — Mark 4 — 10

TRX Hamstring Curl — R1m
TRX Hamstring Curl — Set 1 — 2-leg — 12
TRX Hamstring Curl — Set 2 — 2-leg — 12

TRX Assisted SL Squat — R1m30s
TRX Assisted SL Squat — Set 1 — low box, 2-hand — 6/side
```

**Reporting:** unchanged line = all reps at that mark at RIR 2; a changed count = actual;
"slack", "hips sagged", "fell forward" on a fallout or press = form failure = set below target.
A mark written differently from the prescription = the user changed it; log the performed mark
and run the ladder from it.

## 9. Safety and setup

- **Anchor test every session:** hang full bodyweight, feet off the floor, 3 s. Anything that
  creaks, shifts or moves the door = no straps today.
- **Door anchor on the pull side only** (door closes toward the user, anchor on the far side),
  locked. Never a door opening toward the user, a hollow door, or a slider.
- **Strap check at every exercise change:** handles level, locking loops seated, no twist.
  Unequal straps are the commonest cause of a shoulder complaint on presses.
- **Never let a strap go slack;** a slack rep ends the set. This keeps fallouts, presses and
  mountain climbers safe.
- **Foot cradles:** heels or laces in, straps outside the ankles, feet set while sitting.
- **Abrasion:** hands slightly wider than the straps; inspect for fraying monthly and at every
  new anchor; retire a frayed strap.
