# Exercise Library

Exercises organized by movement pattern. Each entry carries its equipment, its **modality
family** (`modality-rotation.md` §1: DB, BB, MC, BW, KB, TRX, BAND), muscle targets and
selection notes. This file is the index; the technique, prerequisites and ladders for the
bodyweight, kettlebell and suspension-trainer families live in their own files:

| Family | File |
|---|---|
| `BW` | `bodyweight-ladders.md` (ladders), `quick-sessions.md` (circuits) |
| `KB` | `kettlebell.md` (full repertoire, technique gates, ladders, S&S, complexes) |
| `TRX` | `suspension-trainer.md` (angle rule, catalogue, ladders) |

Flag notes below are generic. The user's own `injury-protocols.md` always wins.

## Exercise Selection Rules

1. **Every workout must include**: at least 1 push + 1 pull pattern
2. **Full body days**: 1 horizontal push, 1 horizontal pull, 1 squat OR hinge, 1 vertical push OR pull, 1-2 accessories
3. **Upper days**: 1 horizontal push, 1 horizontal pull, 1 vertical push, 1 vertical pull, 1-2 arm accessories
4. **Lower days**: 1 squat pattern, 1 hinge pattern, 1-2 accessories (calves, core, leg isolation)
5. **Push days**: 2 push patterns (1 horizontal, 1 vertical), tricep accessory
6. **Pull days**: 2 pull patterns (1 horizontal, 1 vertical), bicep accessory
7. **Leg days**: 1 squat, 1 hinge, 2-3 leg accessories
8. **Carries** (added 2026-10-18): one carry per week somewhere in the rotation is the default
   (pull or leg day, after the main work, before forearm isolation). It is an accessory for
   rotation and volume purposes, counted 0.5× to traps and core.
9. **Modality coverage** (added 2026-10-18): each day type's accessory list spans at least 2
   modality families; see `modality-rotation.md` §2. A location with only one family (straps
   only, kettlebells only) is exempt while training there.
10. **Family files win on technique.** Where this table and `kettlebell.md` /
    `suspension-trainer.md` / `bodyweight-ladders.md` disagree on prerequisites or
    progression, the family file wins.
